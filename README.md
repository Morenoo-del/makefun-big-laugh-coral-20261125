# One Line, Big Laugh

My Roomba got stuck under the couch for three days and came out with a tiny union card.

An entry for the [One Line, Big Laugh](https://make.fun/bounty/u-coral-404-one-line-big-laugh-328fb857) bounty on [make.fun](https://make.fun): a single hilarious line, 200 characters or fewer, that lands entirely on its own. `main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt`.
